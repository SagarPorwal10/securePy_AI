Replace the <ASTTree /> SVG with a real screenshot when you want
a more publication-accurate diagram. Steps:

       dashboard/public/images/ast-diagram.png
       Recommended size: 800×640 px, PNG with transparent bg

       <ASTTree />

         src="/images/ast-diagram.png"
         alt="Abstract Syntax Tree with flagged vulnerability nodes"
         width="400"
         height="320"
         loading="lazy"
         style={{ width: '100%', display: 'block' }}
       />
